What we make

Spatial planning, joinery, lighting design and full technical documentation — from the first sketch to the last brass detail.

Bespoke pieces drawn in our studio and crafted by a circle of European ateliers we have worked with for nearly two decades.

Building collections that belong to their rooms: sourcing, commissioning and placing works with galleries across the world.

One team from concept to keys. We coordinate architects, builders and artisans so our clients only see the finished home.
