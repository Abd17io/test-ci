( The Studio )

A house of
quiet things

Founded in Paris in 2007, Maison Noir is a studio of twenty-four architects, designers and makers working across Europe and the Gulf.

We believe a room should be felt before it is seen — in the weight of a door, the temperature of stone under bare feet, the way evening light settles on a linen wall.
