Rooms that breathe.

A Copenhagen studio designing calm, honest homes — natural materials, soft northern light and furniture made to outlive us.

See our homes ↗

01 — Philosophy

We believe a home should quiet the mind. So we begin with light, add only honest materials, and leave room for life to fill the rest.

Light

We study how daylight moves through each room across a year before we place a single wall.

Material

Wood, stone, wool, linen, clay. Surfaces that soften with use and are kind to the planet.

Proportion

Calm comes from measure — ceiling heights, sightlines and the rhythm of things placed with care.
